## E-Commerce Design Priority Rationale

The e-commerce surface (catalog + cart + orders + wallet) is the **primary business value** of the app. The diagnostic terminal aesthetic (DESIGN.md) explains the brand, but **ECOMMERCE_DESIGN.md** is the actual UX spec for buying.

**Treat ECOMMERCE_DESIGN.md as sui for all user-facing surfaces:**
- ItemsPage, ItemDetailPage, CartPage, OrdersPage, OrderDetailPage, WalletPage
- These pages must use the `.ecom-*`, `.cart-*`, `.order-*` class tokens defined in ECOMMERCE_DESIGN.md
- The Auth pages (login, register, verify-otp) can use a simpler, smaller terminal card style (`.auth-card` terminal variant)
